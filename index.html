Where do I get the file?
  1. Go to 20 Weekly Meals and press View More.
  2. Select View Statement.
  3. Download the CSV.

A statement only covers one term, so grab each one you have and drop them all in together — they get merged into a single year.

What does it read?

A CSV with a Date, Description, Amount column — the export from the dining plan page. Credits (Automated Reset, Deposit) are skipped; every negative amount is a swipe, and the hall comes from the description. Balance is used to work out your weekly allowance: it settles on the same number after every reset, which is a truer read on what you were given than summing the top-up amounts.
